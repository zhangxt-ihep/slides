# Public Provenance

- Maps use Natural Earth 1:110m land data, used as approximate geographic context. The source data is kept in `slidev/data/ne_110m_land.geojson`.
- FTS, JUNO, HERD, and CEPC logos remain unmodified for identification. Rights remain with their respective projects; see `slidev/public/logos/SOURCES.md`.
- Dashboard images are browser captures from the IHEP Grafana dashboards. Their source URLs and scope are documented in `slidev/public/DASHBOARD-SOURCES.md`.
- Public FTS and Slidev references are listed in the source links retained by the deck. Raw query responses, internal inventories, and private preparation notes are not distributed.
